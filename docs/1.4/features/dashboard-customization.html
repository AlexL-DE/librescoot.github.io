Dashboard Customization

Light and dark themes, configurable indicators and clock visibility.

Display themes

Choose a light or dark theme in the settings menu. Brightness adjusts to ambient light.

Configurable elements

Control what shows on your dashboard:

  • Clock visibility: show or hide the time; GPS sets it once a fix is available
  • Battery display mode: percentage or range
  • Status icons: toggle individual indicators on or off
  • Map rendering: vector or raster tiles

The scooter keeps time across Hibernation using its internal backup power. GPS can synchronise the clock without a SIM or internet connection. After a complete power loss, including the internal batteries, the clock needs GPS or internet time synchronisation again.

Milestone celebrations

When enabled, the dashboard marks 10 km, then each 100 km. A short notice appears when you cross a milestone; optional banners and confetti appear when you next park.

In the Appearance settings, choose Milestones: Off, Regular, or Regular + Easter eggs. Milestone presentation selects Small notice, Banner, or Banner + confetti. Milestones are off by default.

lsc settings set dashboard.milestones.mode all
lsc settings set dashboard.milestones.presentation banner-and-confetti

Settings

Change dashboard settings in the on-screen menu (double-tap the left brake), on lsd's Settings page, or with lsc:

lsc settings list                          # See all settings
lsc settings set dashboard.map.type offline  # Switch to offline maps
lsc settings set dashboard.theme dark        # Dark theme
